E4. G3 is a uniform-cost diagnostic, not household HESI validation
------------------------------------------------------------------
G3 compares occupied ACS income to the single R2.4 mean $2,894.90805340822/mo.
It does not use household-specific food, PUMA rent, or transport.
A household-specific backtest is designed in
research/september2026_income_nowcast/src/household_specific_adequacy.py and is
NOT nationally executed. No September 2026 nowcast.

E7. CES AWE is not annual-mean AHE times annual-mean hours
----------------------------------------------------------
M4 uses CES0500000011 (all-employee SA weekly earnings). CES0500000003 is
all-employee SA hourly earnings, not production/nonsupervisory.
The 2023→2024 AWE ratio is not identical to AHE ratio × hours ratio.
See CES_SERIES_NOTE.md.

E8. Housing partnership / bedroom rule
--------------------------------------
Partners are RELSHIPP in {21,22,23,24} only. Bedroom count uses
adults − min(partners, 1) + ceil(children/2). Additional partners beyond one
do not add rooms; other adult relatives each count as needing a bedroom.
Not a certified occupancy standard.

E9. Food evidence / infant treatment
------------------------------------
R2.4 joint model excludes households with AGEP==0 (infants). The 2024 observed-
housing pilot prices age 0 with the age-1 USDA cell. Those are different
research objects. Mainland Thrifty cells are used nationally, including AK/HI.

E10. Source counts
------------------
ACS household money sources in the nowcast reconstruction are the eight PUMS
fields WAGP, SEMP, INTP, SSP, SSIP, RETP, PAP, OIP plus a residual to HINCP.
BEA personal interest is not ACS INTP. SSA COLA is not ACS SSP.

E11. HUD effective dates
------------------------
FY2026 FMRs take effect 2025-10-01. The R2.4 table is the revised FY2026 file
effective 2026-05-21. September 2026 therefore uses the revised FY2026
schedule, not the October 2025 original. FY2024 revised (Honolulu, 2024-03-11)
and FY2025 original (2024-10-01) belong to the matched-year cost inputs, not
to the frozen R2.4 geography file.

E14. FY2025 revised FMR: April 27 vs April 28
---------------------------------------------
The Federal Register revised FY2025 FMR notice (90 FR 14158, published
2025-03-28) is effective **2025-04-28**. HUD USER posts the revised county
and Small Area FMR workbooks under the heading “Effective April 27, 2025”.
Prior HESI protocol text used 2025-04-27. That is a documentation discrepancy,
not a change to the calendar-2024 day-weight (FY2025 revised is after
2024-12-31 in either reading). Historical matched-year CSVs are not rewritten.

Westchester County FMR mapping alternatives are listed in
research/historical_matched_baseline/WESTCHESTER_FMR_ALTERNATIVES.md.
No alternative is selected in this repair.
