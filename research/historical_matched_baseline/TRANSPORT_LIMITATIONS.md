# Transportation limitations — 2024 matched-year inputs

Updating the gasoline price **does not** establish a historically accurate 2024 vehicle-cost standard.

R2.4 assigned every included household one car costing **$7,264.70/year** ($605.391667/month). That total is:

- Fuel: 12,000 miles / 32 mpg × $4.35475 (EIA U.S. regular, September 2026 weekly mean) = **$1,633.03**
- Remainder: **$5,631.67** for depreciation, insurance, maintenance, registration, financing, and other items

Those remainder dollars were already labeled hypothetical in the September 2026 pillar. They are not ACS vehicle expenditures, BLS CPI components, or a 2024 insurance quote.

For 2024 matched-year **inputs**:

- Gasoline is replaced with EIA series `EMM_EPMR_PTE_NUS_DPG` calendar-2024 published annual **$3.304/gal** (hashed official annual workbook).
- Fuel becomes 12,000 / 32 × 3.304 = **$1,239.00**
- Remainder is **held at $5,631.67**
- Labeled transport total = **$6,870.67/year**

This total is a **gasoline-updated scenario with frozen non-fuel assumptions**, not a validated 2024 mobility adequacy standard. One-car assignment is unchanged and is still not household vehicle ownership.

Do not describe $6,870.67 as “2024 AAA / IRS / NHTS cost of driving.”
