# Owner housing methodology (research track)

This note is **non-blocking** for six-component engineering on `dev/hesi-2026-framework`.

## Current production joint behavior

`core_r24` joint scenarios apply **replacement rental benchmarks** to occupied households regardless of tenure, including owners. That is documented in frozen `national_R22` audits as a normative cost, not observed owner carrying costs.

## Open research items

- Mortgage principal/interest vs imputed rent vs hybrid owner-specific normative standard
- Geographic alignment of owner costs (county/PUMA) vs renter FMR table
- Tenure-specific hardship interpretation vs essential-cost vector consistency

No code changes to frozen `national_R22` outputs are implied by this track.
