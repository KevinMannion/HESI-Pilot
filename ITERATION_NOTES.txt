Known limitations
-----------------
- 18.9918% is a September 2026 modeled cost versus 2024 ACS gross income scenario.
  Do not publish it as a measured September 2026 hardship prevalence.
- Housing is HUD replacement rent, not observed ACS rent/owner cost.
- Transport assigns one $7,264.70/year vehicle to every included household.
- 90% intervals are ACS sampling-only.
- Infant-containing households and households needing >4 bedrooms are excluded.
- puma_fmr_screened.csv is a frozen research product. Original HUD FY2026 FMR and
  SAFMR workbooks were not used to regenerate it in this run.
- source_2024_2026.csv is a frozen curated USDA table. Original USDA plan workbooks
  were not used to regenerate it in this run.
- Therefore upstream source-to-result reproducibility is NOT complete.
- Scientific validation is NOT complete.
