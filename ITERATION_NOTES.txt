What R2.4 income uses versus what exists in PUMS
------------------------------------------------

Available on 2024 ACS person PUMS (dictionary 2025-10-16): WAGP wages;
SEMP self-employment; RETP retirement (bundled); SSP Social Security;
SSIP SSI; INTP interest/dividends/net rent; PAP public assistance; OIP other;
PERNP earnings; PINCP person total; plus WKHP, WKWN, INDP, COW, allocation flags.
ADJINC 1-year factor 1.015250. HINCP is rolling past-12-month money income,
bottom-coded at -59999.

Proposed method (not implemented)
---------------------------------
Decompose 2024 HINCP into person sources; apply source-specific factors;
hold 2024 household composition and R2.4 eligibility fixed.
Wages: CES/ECI rate × hours × employment, industry-specific; two-stage via ASEC 2025
then Jan–Sep 2026 CES.
Self-employment: BEA proprietors' income, not CES.
SS/SSI: statutory COLA path only.
Retirement and capital: ASEC-informed split; freeze + sensitivity if unsplit.
Public assistance / other / residual: freeze unless a documented program path exists.
Forbidden: uniform CPI on HINCP; CES AHE on total household income.
Output names must be income_month_2026sep_NOWCAST and
share_income_below_three_costs_2026sep_NOWCAST.
