Mandatory interpretation
------------------------
EXECUTED_BY_AUTHOR means the author ran a calculation successfully. It does not establish third-party reproduction.
CLEAN_ENVIRONMENT_REPRODUCED / INTERNALLY_REPRODUCED means a subsequent execution regenerated published numbers from identified inputs on a clean environment.
EXTERNALLY_INDEPENDENTLY_REPRODUCED requires an independent operator on a separate environment, with commands, input hashes, outputs, and a report attached. Until then: NOT_CONFIRMED.
SCIENTIFICALLY_VALIDATED is separate from computational reproducibility. National adequacy and hardship claims remain unvalidated unless documented otherwise.
A passing SHA-256 gate guarantees bytes match a recorded manifest, not scientific correctness.
No release may be called complete if its documented acceptance tests cannot be run from the distribution with its documented public inputs.
